## Before adding an image

1. Confirm the license allows commercial use.
2. Add a row here and an entry in `src/data/credits.json` (this feeds the credits list on `/disclaimer/`).
3. No photos of people presented as staff or customers, nothing graphic, nothing from another restoration company.
